# Venture Pipeline Management System (VPMS)

### Organisation: Mekong Inclusive Ventures (MIV)

---

## Overview

The Venture Pipeline Management System (VPMS) is a digital platform designed to support Mekong Inclusive Ventures (MIV) in managing venture submissions, evaluations, and progress tracking through a centralised and structured workflow.

The platform replaces fragmented manual processes with a streamlined, role-based system that improves visibility, consistency, and operational efficiency across the venture lifecycle across both desktop and mobile experiences.

👉 See full details: [Project Overview](./docs/0-overview/project-overview.md)

---

## Problem Statement

MIV previously relied on dispersed tools and manual workflows, resulting in:
- Limited visibility across venture progress
- Inconsistent review processes
- Increased administrative overhead
- Difficulty tracking submissions and stakeholder feedback

---

## Proposed Solution

VPMS provides:
- Centralised venture data and document management
- Structured submission and review workflows
- Clear visibility into venture progress
- Role-based access control and permissions
- Improved collaboration between stakeholders
- Responsive and mobile-focused interface designs for improved accessibility

---

## Trimester Focus

This trimester focuses on evolving VPMS into a more accessible, scalable, and deployment-ready prototype.

### Key Priorities
- External hosting readiness
- Simplified authentication and SSO exploration
- Improved usability for users with low digital literacy
- Accessibility and UI enhancements
- Mobile interface refinement and responsiveness
- Refinement of submission and review workflows
- Increased testing and validation coverage

## Project Context

This project is being developed as part of a capstone initiative in collaboration with Mekong Inclusive Ventures (MIV). Development is guided by the provided Product Requirements Documentation (PRD), stakeholder feedback, and previous handover materials.

The current prototype serves as a foundation for continued development, stakeholder evaluation, mobile accessibility improvements, and future deployment readiness.
