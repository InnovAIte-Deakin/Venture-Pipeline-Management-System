# VPMS General FAQ

## 1. What is VPMS?
VPMS (Venture Pipeline Management System) is a platform used to manage and track businesses applying for funding or support. It helps organise venture information, monitor readiness, and support connections between entrepeneurs and financial institutions.

---

## 2. Who is VPMS for?

- Small business owners (MSMEs)
- Impact users with limited digital experience
- Admins managing applications
- Organisations providing funding/support

---

## 3. What can I do on VPMS?
- Submit a business application
- Upload documents
- Track application progress
- Communicate with reviewers

---

## 4. How much funding can I apply for?
Typically between **$250 and $10,000**, depending on the program.

---

## 5. Do I need business experience to apply?
No. VPMS is designed to support early-stage and informal businesses.

---

## 6. What documents are required?
Only simple documents are required (see onboarding FAQ for details). The system is designed to minimise barriers.

---

## 7. Can I save my application and come back later?
Yes. Applications can be saved as drafts before submission.

---

## 8. How do I know my application status?
You can track your progress through stages such as:
- Submitted
- Under Review
- Approved / Rejected

---

## 9. Is my data secure?
Yes. VPMS follows secure data handling practices to protect your personal and business information.

---

## 10. Who reviews my application?
Applications are typically reviewed by MIV staff and relevant parters who assess readiness and dtermine eligibility for funding support.

---

## 11. What happens after approval?
If approved:
- You may receive funding
- You may receive additional support (mentoring, guidance)

---

## 12. What if my application is rejected?
You may:
- Receive feedback
- Be encouraged to reapply later

---

## 13. Can admins edit my application?
Admins may assist or request changes, but core data remains user-controlled where possible.

---

## 14. Do I need to use VPMS myself, or will someone assist me?
MIV staff or trained facilitators may assist users by collecting and entering information into the system.

---

## 15. Can VPMS be used on mobile devices?
Yes, VPMS is designed to be accessible via web browsers and may be used on mobile devices.

---

## 16. Why is my information being collected?
Your information improves access to financial services for underserved communities.
